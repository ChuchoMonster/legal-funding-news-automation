## KPMG Appoints First U.S. Legal Services Chief as Arizona Alternative Business Structure Faces Scrutiny

KPMG has named Christian Athanasoulas as the first head of KPMG US Legal Services, one year after becoming the first Big Four firm approved to operate a U.S. law firm under Arizona's alternative business structure rules.

The division focuses on integrating legal services — such as post-merger contract work and entity dissolution — with KPMG's existing corporate advisory platform.

The appointment arrives amid growing regulatory scrutiny of Arizona's ABS program, with state officials recommending rule changes to ensure firms serve Arizona clients directly.
