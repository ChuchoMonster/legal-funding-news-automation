Subject: Gen Re Calls for EU-Wide TPLF Regulation, Social Media Trial Jury Deliberates, and This Week's Funding Roundup

Hey folks,

The reinsurance industry is stepping into the litigation funding debate. Gen Re published a detailed analysis this week arguing that the EU needs a [unified regulatory framework for third-party litigation funding](https://legalfundingjournal.com/gen-re-calls-for-eu-wide-third-party-litigation-funding-regulation/) — citing more than 300 funders operating across Europe with limited transparency and fragmented oversight.

Meanwhile, jury deliberations continue in the landmark social media addiction trial backed by Flashlight Capital. The [bellwether case against Meta and YouTube](https://legalfundingjournal.com/meta-and-google-pivot-defense-in-funded-social-media-addiction-trial-to-plaintiffs-personal-history/) could set a major precedent for funder involvement in consumer protection mega-litigation, with over 1,600 related lawsuits waiting in the wings.

On the corporate side, [Counsel Financial made two strategic hires](https://legalfundingjournal.com/counsel-financial-appoints-two-directors-to-support-portfolio-growth-and-capital-provider-solutions/) to expand its plaintiff law firm financing operations, while a bankruptcy trustee moved to [sell Art Van Furniture's Visa/Mastercard interchange litigation rights](https://legalfundingjournal.com/art-van-furniture-chapter-7-trustee-seeks-court-approval-to-sell-visa-mastercard-interchange-litigation-rights-for-850000/) for $850,000 — highlighting the growing market for claims as tradeable assets.

And in Australia, the court ruled against an [LCM-funded client in a commercial dispute](https://legalfundingjournal.com/australian-court-rules-against-litigation-capital-management-client/), sending the funder's shares lower. A reminder that even in a booming industry, the underlying risk-reward dynamics remain front and center.

- The social media addiction trial verdict could drop any day — a plaintiff win would likely accelerate funder interest in the consumer protection mass tort space.
- California's proposed litigation funding regulations are advancing through committee — watch for markup language and industry reaction.
- The EU regulatory conversation is heating up after Gen Re's publication — expect more insurer and reinsurer voices joining the call for harmonized rules.
